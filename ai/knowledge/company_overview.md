# ECOVAL Sustainable Materials — Company Overview

## Mission
ECOVAL Sustainable Materials is a Vietnamese circular economy startup dedicated to transforming plastic waste and agricultural byproducts into sustainable, high-performance building materials. Our flagship product — the Heritage Breeze Block — upcycles multi-layer plastic packaging and rice husk into decorative ventilation blocks that reduce CO2 emissions and support Vietnam's EPR compliance framework.

## Achievements & Certifications
1. **Giải Ba EPICS 8 (ASU & Dow)**: Third place in the EPICS 8 competition organized by Arizona State University and Dow Chemical. Recognized for engineering solutions with community impact.
2. **Giải Khuyến khích VWRA 2025**: Encouragement Award from the Vietnam Waste Recovery Association (VWRA) 2025 for innovation in waste-to-value technology.
3. **Đồng hành & Ứng dụng Thực tế (Ánh Thủy JSC)**: Certified real-world application partnership with Ánh Thủy Environment and Services Equipment Technology Joint Stock Company, validating ECOVAL's technology in industrial settings.

## Ecosystem Partners
- **Ánh Thủy Environment (Anh Thủy JSC)**: Environmental services and equipment technology partner, providing real-world application validation
- **Thanh Tùng 2**: Material supply chain and logistics partner
- **HCMUT R&D Lab**: Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology research laboratory providing testing, validation, and material science expertise

## Team
ECOVAL has a team of 7 members working across materials science, engineering, business development, and technology.

## Technology Platform
- **Web Application**: Full-stack Next.js application with ESG/EPR calculators, partner registration forms, and an admin dashboard
- **Backend**: FastAPI (Python) with SQLAlchemy, encrypted data storage, and automated email notifications
- **ESG Calculator**: Real-time environmental impact predictions based on brick quantity or installation area
- **EPR Cashflow Engine**: Financial modeling tool showing companies how ECOVAL partnerships reduce their EPR fee obligations
