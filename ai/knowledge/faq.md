# ECOVAL — Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

### What is ECOVAL?
ECOVAL Circular Materials is a Vietnamese green technology startup that produces eco-friendly heritage breeze blocks (gạch bông gió di sản) upcycling multi-layer plastic waste (MLP), rice husk (vỏ trấu), and coconut coir (xơ dừa). Each block upcycles ~0.60 kg of MLP plastic waste and reduces 1.5 kg of CO2 emissions.

### What materials are used in ECOVAL breeze blocks?
ECOVAL blocks are made from multi-layer plastic waste (MLP), agricultural biomass by-products (rice husk and coconut coir fibers), and eco-friendly inorganic pigments/cross-linking additives. Coconut coir and rice husk provide natural fiber reinforcement, high thermal resistance, low water absorption, and environmental durability.

### What is the material ratio formula for ECOVAL blocks?
- **Standard Composite Matrix**: 60-70% MLP plastic waste + 25-30% Rice husk & Coconut coir fibers + 5-10% Additives (~0.60 kg MLP per block).
- **Generation 3 Commercial Heritage Blocks**: 45% MLP plastic + 45% Rice husk & Coconut coir fibers + 10% Inorganic minerals & heritage pigments for optimal aerodynamic strength and colorfast weatherability.
