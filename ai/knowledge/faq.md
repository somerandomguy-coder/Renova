# RENOVA — Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

### What is RENOVA?
RENOVA Circular is a Vietnamese startup that produces eco-friendly breeze blocks (gạch bông gió) from recycled plastic waste and rice husk. Each brick upcycles 1.05 kg of plastic waste and reduces 1.50 kg of CO2 emissions.

### What makes RENOVA different from traditional breeze blocks?
Traditional breeze blocks are made from concrete (cement + sand + aggregate). RENOVA blocks use 70% recycled plastic waste and 25% rice husk instead, with only 5% additives. This means:
- Every brick diverts plastic from landfills and oceans
- Lower carbon footprint than concrete production
- Comparable or superior compressive strength (up to 8.2 MPa for Gen3)
- Better thermal insulation — hollow design reduces indoor temperatures by 3-4°C
- UV resistant with 10-year outdoor color warranty

### How strong are RENOVA bricks?
RENOVA's Generation 3 Heritage Breeze Blocks have a compressive strength of 7.8 - 8.2 MPa, which is twice the endurance of standard autoclaved aerated concrete blocks. They meet or exceed Vietnamese construction standards for non-load-bearing partition walls and decorative facades.

### Are RENOVA bricks waterproof?
RENOVA bricks have very low water absorption — less than 1.2% for the Generation 3 Heritage blocks. This makes them suitable for both indoor and outdoor applications, including exterior facades exposed to rain.

### Are RENOVA bricks safe for outdoor use?
Yes. All RENOVA products are tested and certified for UV resistance. Generation 3 blocks carry a 10-year outdoor warranty for colorfastness. They have passed SGS 500-hour UV chamber testing and ASTM G154 certification.

## Business & Ordering

### How much does a RENOVA brick cost?
The estimated retail price for RENOVA Heritage Breeze Blocks is approximately 45,000 VND per piece. Bulk pricing is available for commercial projects.

### How many bricks do I need for my project?
You need approximately 25 RENOVA breeze blocks per 1 m² of wall surface area (based on 19×19×6.5 cm block dimensions). For example:
- 10 m² wall = ~250 bricks
- 40 m² wall = ~1,000 bricks
- 100 m² wall = ~2,500 bricks

## EPR & Sustainability

### What is EPR and how does RENOVA help?
EPR (Extended Producer Responsibility) is a Vietnamese regulation requiring companies that produce or import packaging to pay for its recycling. RENOVA helps companies offset their EPR obligations by:
1. Using their plastic waste as raw material for bricks
2. Providing documented recycling certificates
3. Offering EPR offset credits when companies purchase RENOVA bricks for their facilities

### How much can a company save on EPR fees with RENOVA?
Companies can achieve up to 40% reduction in their EPR fee obligations. For example, a company releasing 5,000 kg of MLP packaging annually could save approximately 30,000,000 VND in EPR fees.

### How do I become an EPR partner?
Fill out the EPR Partner Registration form on the RENOVA website. You'll need to provide your company name, contact information, annual plastic waste volume, and whether you need EPR certification.

## Technical

### What types of plastic does RENOVA accept?
RENOVA primarily processes Multi-Layer Plastic (MLP) — the most difficult type of plastic to recycle. This includes:
- Food packaging films
- Multi-layer sachets and pouches
- Laminated packaging materials
- LDPE and HDPE films

### Can RENOVA bricks be cut or drilled?
Yes, RENOVA blocks can be cut with standard masonry tools and accept standard fasteners. However, due to the polymer matrix, they may require slightly different cutting techniques compared to concrete blocks.

### What is the lifespan of RENOVA bricks?
RENOVA bricks are designed for long-term durability. The polymer-fiber composite is resistant to moisture, UV degradation, and biological attack (mold, termites). Expected lifespan exceeds 25 years under normal outdoor conditions.
