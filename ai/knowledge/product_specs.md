# ECOVAL Circular Materials — Product Specifications

## Overview
ECOVAL produces eco-friendly heritage breeze blocks (gạch bông gió di sản) engineered from upcycled multi-layer plastic waste (MLP), rice husk (vỏ trấu), and coconut coir (xơ dừa) fibers.

## Technical Specifications
- **Standard Dimensions (Kích thước tiêu chuẩn)**: 200 × 200 × 60 mm (also available in 190 × 190 × 65 mm), standard unit weight ~1.5 kg per block.
- **Compressive Strength (Cường độ chịu nén)**: 7.8 – 8.2 MPa (certified load capacity exceeding standard unbaked brick requirements).
- **Weight Efficiency**: 20–30% lighter than traditional concrete/clay blocks.
- **MLP Plastic Waste Content**: ~0.60 kg MLP plastic packaging upcycled per block.
- **Natural Fiber Reinforcement**: Rice husk (vỏ trấu) & Coconut coir (xơ dừa) fibers for enhanced flexural toughness and active thermal insulation.
- **Carbon Profile**: At least -1 kg net carbon footprint, 1.5 kg CO2eq reduction per block (saving 0.3–0.6 tons CO2eq per 1,000 blocks).
- **Water Absorption**: Extremely low water absorption (< 1.8%).
- **Thermal & Acoustic Performance**: High thermal resistance (Thermal Shield) and noise insulation.
- **Weather & UV Warranty**: 10-year anti-aging & UV colorfastness assurance.
- **EPR Obligation Reduction**: FMCG brand partners contract upcycling with ECOVAL to receive up to 40% EPR fee reduction and verified digital ESG/EPR certificates.

## Material Composition Ratios
- **Standard Density Matrix**: 60–70% Multi-Layer Plastic (MLP) + 25–30% Rice Husk & Coconut Coir Fibers + 5–10% Additives.
- **Generation 3 Commercial Heritage Breeze Block**: 45% Multi-Layer Plastic (MLP) + 45% Rice Husk & Coconut Coir Fibers + 10% Inorganic Minerals & Heritage Pigments.
